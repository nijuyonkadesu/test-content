Daigo Fubuki, codename **F1**, is the reconstructed identity of the
[[rounders|Rounders]] field commander for the Far East sector — the tier
of the hierarchy *above* the handlers, the man to whom "FB" reported. He
never appears on screen. His existence is inferred entirely from the
recovered message logs of the people beneath him, which makes this page
the wiki's most heavily disclaimed biography and, editors would argue,
its most honest picture of how [[sern|SERN]] actually works.

:::warning
**Authenticity note.** Every fact on this page is a reconstruction from
the FB message archive and two SOC routing headers recovered in
[[operation-verdandi]]. The name "Daigo Fubuki" itself may be another
alias in a chain of them. Treat the infobox as a hypothesis with a
picture. #lore #stub
:::

::section[The collector]{icon="🗄️"}

F1's traffic shows one obsession: vintage computers. The IBN 5100
retrieval order that drives half the Alpha arc originates from his desk,
but it sits in a longer pattern — recovered manifests show SOC teams
quietly acquiring pre-1980 hardware across three continents since at
least 2005.[^manifest] The lab's eventual theory: [[sern|SERN]] was not
looking for *the* IBN 5100. It was looking for ==all of them==, because
any surviving unit could decode what the lab decoded.

The single failure on the table is the plot of the series.

::section[Command style]{icon="📡"}

:::::columns{ratio="55-45"}
::::column
Read end to end, F1's side of the FB correspondence is a portrait of
institutional evil at its most banal: no threats, no ideology, just
deadlines. Assets are "inventory." The order to cut [[moeka-kiryu|M4]]
loose is eleven words long and misspells her codename. The wiki
reproduces none of the messages directly — the archive's legal status is
disputed — but the pattern they document is why this page exists.
