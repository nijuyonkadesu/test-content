Nae Tennouji is the young daughter of [[yuugo-tennouji|Mr. Braun]], raised
in the CRT shop under the [[future-gadget-lab|Future Gadget Lab]] and
functionally adopted as the building's little sister. She trails after
[[mayuri|Mayuri]], tolerates [[okabe|Okabe's]] theatrics with a
politeness beyond her years, and sees — because children in shops see
everything — considerably more of the building's secrets than anyone
credits.

::section[What Nae knows]{icon="👀"}

:::note
This section is deliberately understated in the anime and easy to miss.
Nae is present, in the background, for at least three scenes where FB's
phone is in frame. The wiki takes no position on what an eleven-year-old
made of it. The [[alpha-worldline|Alpha]] visual-novel route takes one.
:::

:::collapse{title="Spoilers: the Alpha route's darkest branch"}
One infamous Alpha branch follows Nae after she pieces together her
father's double life and its ending. The branch fast-forwards fifteen
years to a Nae who has joined the [[rounders|Rounders]] herself — not out
of loyalty to SERN but to get close enough to the people who used her
father to return the favor. It is the franchise's bleakest ending, and the
strongest argument in the text that ==the Rounders system is
self-perpetuating by design==: it manufactures the orphans it later
recruits. #lore
:::

::section[Life in the building]{icon="🏢"}

:::::columns{ratio="55-45"}
::::column
The Tennouji apartment, [[braun-tsuushin|the shop]], and the
[[future-gadget-lab|lab]] share one staircase, one breaker panel, and no
soundproofing whatsoever. Nae's childhood is therefore scored by the
building itself: the [[phone-microwave|Phone Microwave]] tripping the
power mid-cartoon, [[okabe|Okabe]] declaiming at the ceiling, her father
bellowing back through the floor. She has learned to read the household's
mood from the electricity — ==three brownouts in an hour means the lab is
excited about something==, which historically means everyone should worry.
::::
