Faris NyanNyan — legal name **Rumiho Akiha** — is the star waitress of the
[[mayqueen-nyannyan|MayQueen NyanNyan]] maid café, Lab Member No. 007, and,
behind the cat-ear persona, the heiress of the family that owns most of the
land under [[akihabara|Akihabara]]. The district's transformation into an
otaku capital was, in large part, a decision she made as a grieving child
with a controlling interest; the moe economy of an entire city block is
downstream of one girl's coping mechanism.[^district]

::section[Two names]{icon="🎭"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="Faris"}
The public self: relentlessly cheerful, fluent in the maid-café idiom she
largely invented, and a national-class player of the collectible card game
*Rai-Net Access Battlers*. "Faris" is not a mask so much as a renovation —
she built the persona at age eleven and moved in permanently.
:::
:::tab{label="Rumiho"}
The private self appears rarely: in board meetings of
[[akiha-cyber-solutions|ACS]] she attends by proxy, in the anniversary of
her father's death, and in the handful of scenes where [[okabe|Okabe]]
needs the person rather than the performance. Rumiho speaks without the
"nya." It is always slightly alarming.
:::
::::

::section[The D-Mail]{icon="📨"}

Faris's [[phone-microwave|D-Mail]] is one of the story's cleanest
demonstrations of worldline mechanics: a single message to the past —
warning her father not to board a plane in 2001 — and [[akihabara|
Akihabara]] itself transforms. On the resulting branch her father lives,
the moe district never happens, the [[radio-kaikan|Radio Kaikan]] hosts
appliance vendors instead of card tournaments, and the IBN 5100 the lab
desperately needs follows a completely different chain of custody.[^ibn]

:::warning
Undoing this D-Mail is among the hardest things [[okabe|Okabe]] does in the
Alpha arc — not technically, but because it requires asking a daughter to
re-consent to her father's death. See [[episode-17]]. Divergence delta for
the branch: $|\Delta d| \approx 0.075$. #lore
:::

::section[Capabilities]{icon="🧪"}

:::::columns{ratio="55-45"}
::::column
It is easy to forget, amid the cat ears, that Faris is one of the most
strategically capable people in the cast. She reads the Rai-Net board the
way [[kurisu|Kurisu]] reads a journal paper, she reads *people* better
than anyone in the lab, and she deduced Okabe's time-travel secret from
behavioral evidence alone — then pretended she hadn't, to spare him the
explanation, nya.
::::
::::column
:::tip
Her Rai-Net national title is not flavor text: in [[episode-11]] the
championship bracket becomes, briefly and absurdly, the fulcrum of a
worldline. Watch her hands in that episode — the animators gave her a
tell.
:::
