Moeka Kiryu is a pale, silent woman who [[okabe|Okabe]] first encounters in
[[akihabara|Akihabara]] while she is hunting for a retro computer — the same
IBN 5100 the lab needs to decode [[sern|SERN's]] internal reports. She types
constantly, answers questions by holding her phone screen up to your face,
and joins the [[future-gadget-laboratory|Future Gadget Laboratory]] as Lab
Member No. 005 with a smile that never once reaches her eyes.

She is also, on the [[alpha-worldline|Alpha worldline]], the field leader of
the [[rounders|Rounders]] unit that raids the lab in [[episode-12]] — the
event that turns the story from comedy into tragedy and sets [[okabe|Okabe]]
on his long war against causality.

::section[The phone]{icon="📱"}

:::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
::::column
Moeka's phone is the entire architecture of her personality. Her handler —
known to her only as **FB** — communicates exclusively through it, and years
of that arrangement have hollowed out every other relationship she has.
When she loses access to FB in one worldline, the collapse that follows is
total: not grief exactly, but the removal of the only load-bearing wall in
a person.

Lab records credit her with the fastest confirmed typing speed of any
member — [[daru|Daru]] clocked her at ==120 words per minute on a numeric
keypad== before she noticed him counting and left the room.
::::
::::column
:::danger
On Alpha-line branches, do not mistake her silence for passivity. The raid
of August 2010 was planned around the lab's own schedule, which means she
had been reporting on it for weeks — as a member.
:::
::::
:::::

::section[History]{icon="📜"}

Little of Moeka's background survives in any verifiable form; the following
is assembled from Rounders personnel fragments recovered by [[daru|Daru]]
and from her own sparse statements. She grew up effectively invisible —
the kind of person teachers forgot to call on — and was recruited into
SERN's [[rounders|Rounders]] program in her late teens, apparently selected
*because* nobody would miss her.[^recruit] FB gave her a codename, a
mission, and a reason to wake up, in that order of importance.

| Period | Known activity |
|---|---|
| ~2005 | First contact with the recruiter later identified as FB |
| 2008 | Formal Rounders induction, codename M4 |
| 2009 | Deep-cover placement as freelance editor |
| 2010-07 | Tasked with locating the IBN 5100 |
| 2010-08 | Joins the lab; leads the raid days later ([[alpha-worldline|Alpha]]) |
| 2010-08 | Neutralized during [[okabe|Okabe's]] D-Mail counter-operations |

::section[Across the worldlines]{icon="🌐"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="Alpha"}
The loyal instrument. She executes the raid, reports the lab's
[[phone-microwave|Phone Microwave]] to SERN, and is discarded by her
employers almost immediately afterward — a detail Okabe only appreciates
much later.
:::
:::tab{label="Beta"}
On [[beta-worldline|Beta]] branches she never receives the order. She
drifts through Akihabara still hunting the IBN 5100 for a handler who has
stopped answering, which may be the crueler fate.
:::
:::tab{label="Steins;Gate"}
A freelance editor with a bad phone habit and no handler at all. She still
doesn't talk much. Some architecture doesn't rebuild just because the war
that built it never happened.
:::
::::

:::collapse{title="Spoilers: the identity of FB"}
FB is [[yuugo-tennouji|Yuugo Tennouji]] — Mr. Braun, the lab's own
landlord, himself a Rounder pressed into service years earlier. The
revelation lands twice: once for the betrayal, and once for the symmetry,
because FB ran Moeka exactly the way SERN ran him — through the one channel
of affection the target could not afford to lose. The name comes from
*Ferdinand Braun*, inventor of the CRT.[^fb]
:::
