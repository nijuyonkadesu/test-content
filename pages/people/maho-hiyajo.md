Maho Hiyajo is a senior researcher at [[viktor-chondria-university|Viktor
Chondria University]], co-creator of the [[amadeus-system|Amadeus]]
memory-model AI, and the person who knew [[kurisu|Kurisu Makise]] best in
the years America had her. On the [[beta-worldline|Beta worldline]] she
arrives in Japan carrying two things: the Amadeus build containing her
dead friend's memories, and a grief she has decided to call a research
program.

::section[Salieri]{icon="🎹"}

Maho's self-mythology runs through [[wp:Antonio Salieri]] — the capable
musician history remembers mostly for standing next to Mozart. She was
Kurisu's *senpai*: older, earlier into the program, and then, permanently,
second. The show treats her framing with more respect than she treats
herself; the text is clear that ==Amadeus exists because Salieri built
it==, and that Kurisu knew exactly which of them could.[^salieri]

:::tip
Watch her hands, not her dialogue. Maho states her positions calmly and
then plays piano like she is trying to break it. The animators used the
piano as her polygraph.
:::

Sent to Japan for the Amadeus lecture circuit alongside
[[alexis-leskinen|Professor Leskinen]], Maho collides with the
[[future-gadget-laboratory|Future Gadget Laboratory]] and with
[[okabe|Okabe]] — the one person whose grief for Kurisu matches hers and
whose reasons cannot be spoken aloud. Their wary alliance becomes the
emotional spine of the Beta arc: two executors of the same estate,
comparing inventories.

She is also, notably, the first outsider to reconstruct the truth of the
[[phone-microwave|Phone Microwave]] from evidence alone — Kurisu's
surviving files, plus the lab's conspicuous silences.[^recon]

::section[Trivia]{icon="✨"}

- Her Amadeus access phrase is a Mozart Köchel number; [[daru|Daru]]
  guessed it in four tries and has never let her forget it.
- Carries Kurisu's actual research notebook — paper, water-stained —
  through every scene where the digital copy is on screen. The show never
  comments on it.
- Coffee order: black, then three sugars added one at a time as the
  meeting gets worse.

[^salieri]: The historical Salieri–Mozart rivalry is itself mostly legend;
    see [[wp:Antonio Salieri]]. Maho, characteristically, knows this and
    identifies with the legend anyway.
[^recon]: Her reconstruction, recovered in supplementary materials, gets
    every load-bearing fact right except one: she assumed the machine had
    to be intentional. Nobody guesses the yogurt.
