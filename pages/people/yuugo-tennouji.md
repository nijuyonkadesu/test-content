Yuugo Tennouji — universally "Mr. Braun," after the [[wp:Karl Ferdinand
Braun|inventor of the CRT]] he refuses to stop selling — is the landlord of
the building that houses the [[future-gadget-lab|Future Gadget Lab]], a
single father to [[nae-tennouji|Nae]], and the loudest fixture of the
[[akihabara|Akihabara]] backstreet the lab calls home. He bellows at
[[okabe|Okabe]] about the rent, the noise, and the microwave that keeps
browning out his shop's power, roughly in that order, roughly daily.

On the [[alpha-worldline|Alpha worldline]] he is also **FB** — the
[[rounders|Rounders]] handler who has been running [[moeka-kiryu|Moeka
Kiryu]] for years from the floor directly beneath the lab he was assigned
to watch.

[[braun-tsuushin|Braun Tsuushin]] sells and repairs cathode-ray tube
televisions exclusively, a business model that stopped making sense around
2003 and that Tennouji defends with the zeal of a man who is either a
romantic or laundering something.[^shop] The truth is a little of both:
the shop is real, the love of the technology is real, and the shop is
*also* how SERN pays him.

He took the work for one reason. Nae's medical bills after her mother's
death would have ended the shop, and SERN's recruiter — as SERN's
recruiters reliably do — arrived within a week of the funeral with exactly
the right number on a piece of paper.

:::collapse{title="Spoilers: the handler beneath the floorboards"}
As FB, Tennouji ran Moeka through text messages composed on an old phone
he kept taped under the shop counter — close enough to the lab's
[[phone-microwave|Phone Microwave]] that at least one D-Mail is believed
to have shifted a worldline while sitting in his outbox. He never met her
face to face until the day the arrangement collapsed, and by every
recovered account he had come to think of her, with genuine and useless
tenderness, as a second daughter.

When SERN ordered him to cut her loose, he obeyed — the same way he had
obeyed for eleven years, because obedience was the price of Nae's future.
The scene where Okabe confronts him is played almost without anger: two
men who both know exactly what SERN costs, itemized.
:::

::section[Assessment]{icon="🧪"}

:::note
The Suzuha connection means Tennouji's bloodline is load-bearing for the
entire [[steins-gate-worldline|Steins Gate]] endgame — a fact precisely
zero people in 2010 are equipped to appreciate. The wiki files him under
People rather than Main Cast, but the plot disagrees. #lore
:::

His genetics gave Suzuha the frame that survives a one-way trip through
time twice; his money, extracted from SERN under duress, kept the building
standing that the lab needed to exist. Rarely has a supporting character
supported so literally.

::section[The shop's finances]{icon="🧾"}

An [[atchannel|@channel]] running joke — "how is that CRT shop still
open?" — turns out to be load-bearing foreshadowing. [[daru|Daru's]]
casual reconstruction of the shop's books in [[episode-15]] finds monthly
deposits from a shell distributor that sells nothing, routed through an
importer that the [[committee-of-300|Committee]] paper trail later
touches. The lesson this wiki draws for readers: ==in this franchise, the
economics are never a plot hole; they are a clue==.
