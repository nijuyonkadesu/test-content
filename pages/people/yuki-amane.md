Yuki Amane is a gentle, unflappable costumer from [[mayuri|Mayuri's]]
cosplay circle — and, per the arithmetic no one in 2010 is allowed to say
out loud, the future wife of [[daru|Itaru Hashida]] and mother of
[[suzuha-amane|Suzuha Amane]]. On [[beta-worldline|Beta]] branches, where
Suzuha operates openly inside the lab, Yuki's introduction produces the
franchise's strangest recurring scene: a soldier from 2036 making shy
small talk with her own mother about wig care.

::section[The arithmetic]{icon="🧮"}

:::note
Suzuha's existence requires this marriage on every worldline that reaches
2036 with a resistance in it. The lab therefore treats Daru and Yuki's
courtship as ==infrastructure== — [[okabe|Okabe]] has personally
intervened in at least two dates, with the desperation of a man protecting
a causal chain rather than a friend's love life. See [[episode-21]] for
the field operation the fandom calls "Operation Arclight." #lore
:::

The pairing looks improbable exactly once — on paper. In person, Yuki's
serenity turns out to be the one force in nature Daru's self-deprecation
cannot outrun. She finds his forum-brain funny. He finds this
incomprehensible. The wiki finds it adorable and canonical.

::section[Craft]{icon="🧵"}

Within the circle, Yuki is the finisher — the one a half-built costume
goes to in the last seventy-two hours before an event. Mayuri's
[[mayqueen-nyannyan|MayQueen]] variants, [[luka-urushibara|Luka's]] more
elaborate commissions, and one legendary full-armor build documented at
[[comima-79]] all carry her seamwork.

::section[Across the worldlines]{icon="🌐"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="Alpha"}
Never appears on screen — on [[alpha-worldline|Alpha]] branches the lab
implodes before the cosplay circle's winter event, and the introduction
that starts everything is never made. Whether Daru and Yuki still find
each other off screen is one of the fandom's favorite unanswerable
questions.
:::
:::tab{label="Beta"}
Fully present: the courtship, "Operation Arclight," and the strange warm
scenes with [[suzuha-amane|Suzuha]] that only the audience can bear the
full weight of. On the darkest Beta branches, the marriage happens in a
world already at war — the resistance's records list her as a supply
officer, which surprises exactly nobody who has watched her run a
seventy-two-hour costume crunch.
:::
:::tab{label="Steins;Gate"}
The good line's version is almost aggressively ordinary: a fabric-shop
part-timer dating a hacker who finally worked up the nerve. The wiki
notes, without further comment, that of every relationship in the cast,
this is the one the [[steins-gate-worldline|Steins Gate worldline]]
changes least.
:::
::::

::section[The circle]{icon="🎪"}

Yuki's cosplay circle — never formally named on screen, listed at event
registration as ==「May Queen's Court」== — overlaps heavily with the
lab's orbit:

- [[mayuri|Mayuri Shiina]] — founder, and the one who recruits Yuki
- [[luka-urushibara|Luka Urushibara]] — reluctant, spectacular model
- [[faris-nyannyan|Faris NyanNyan]] — patron; lends the
  [[mayqueen-nyannyan|café]] for fittings after hours
- Two members known only by circle handles, tracked at
  [[may-queens-court]] #stub

::section[Trivia]{icon="✨"}

- Suzuha, a combat engineer who can field-strip a time machine, cannot
  thread a needle. Yuki teaches her, patiently, in one quiet Beta-line
  scene that the fandom refuses to stop citing.
- Her train commute passes [[akihabara|Akihabara]] station at 8:02; Daru
  planned an entire week around learning this and then failed to board
  the train. Twice.
- Tea over coffee; hems by hand even when the machine is faster; has
  never once raised her voice on screen.
