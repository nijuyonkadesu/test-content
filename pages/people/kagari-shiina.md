Kagari Shiina is a war orphan from the [[beta-worldline|Beta worldline's]]
ruined future, adopted there by an adult [[mayuri|Mayuri Shiina]] and sent
back to 1998 aboard the resistance's second time machine. The insertion
goes wrong; the lab meets her twelve years later as an amnesiac young
woman in 2010 [[akihabara|Akihabara]] who remembers nothing but a fragment
of melody — a lullaby Mayuri has hummed since childhood.

::section[The lost years]{icon="🧩"}

Between 1998 and 2010 Kagari's trail goes almost completely dark; the wiki
reconstructs it from the Beta arc's worst reveals. The short version: a
child with no records, no guardian, and a time machine's residue on her
clothes does not stay unnoticed by the kind of institutions that watch for
exactly that.[^trail]

:::collapse{title="Spoilers: what the Strategic Focus Lab did"}
Kagari's amnesia is not trauma alone — it is product. The
[[strategic-focus-lab|SFL]] program under [[alexis-leskinen|Leskinen]]
identified her as time-machine debris and spent years conditioning her as
a sleeper asset, using the [[amadeus-system|Amadeus]] tooling in reverse:
instead of instantiating a recorded mind, ==overwriting a live one==. The
lullaby is the seam where the overwrite failed. Every scene where she
hums it is the real Kagari surfacing, which reframes the entire arc on
rewatch and is the reason this wiki's editors consider her the Beta
storyline's most quietly horrifying element. #lore
:::

::section[Mother and daughter]{icon="🎀"}

:::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
::::column
The heart of Kagari's story is anachronistic: she is the adopted daughter
of a Mayuri who does not exist yet, meeting the eighteen-year-old who will
become her. The 2010 Mayuri, without a word of the future explained to
her, simply *behaves like her mother* — same warmth, same stubbornness on
exactly one subject at a time — and Kagari's conditioning erodes against
it. The show's thesis in miniature: identity is not storage, it is
practice.
::::

::section[The lullaby]{icon="🎼"}

The melody itself — four bars, minor key, resolving on an unresolved
fourth — appears in the score every time conditioning and memory fight
for the same body. Musicologically-minded fans have transcribed it and
noted that Mayuri hums it fractionally differently in 2010 than adult
Mayuri does in 2036 flashbacks: the 2036 version is the *taught* version,
smoothed by repetition to a child. The 2010 version still has the
hesitation in bar three. Kagari, devastatingly, hums the smoothed one.[^hum]

:::tip
First-time viewers should not look up the lullaby's title — it is named
on screen only at the arc's climax, and the name is a spoiler in four
syllables.
:::
