Judy Reyes is the research ethicist attached to [[viktor-chondria-university|
Viktor Chondria University's]] [[amadeus-system|Amadeus]] project — the
person whose signature was required, and obtained, before a dead woman's
memories were switched on and asked how they felt today. A minor figure
in the Beta arc's background, she has become a fixture of the fandom's
supplementary-material deep dives, because her audit memos, glimpsed on
screen for single frames, reward pausing.[^frames]

::section[The Reyes Criteria]{icon="⚖️"}

Her published framework for memory-model research asks three questions of
any Amadeus-class instantiation, and the show quietly grades its cast
against them:

| Criterion | Amadeus-Kurisu | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Did the source consent to instantiation? | Research-archive clause, signed at 17 | Technically |
| Can the instance refuse interaction? | Sessions are operator-initiated | No |
| Who may terminate, and on what grounds? | [[strategic-focus-lab\|SFL]] directorate, at discretion | The problem |

The third row is the arc's ethical hinge: ==everyone who loves the
instance has no authority over it, and everyone with authority does not
love it==.

::section[In the story]{icon="🎬"}

:::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
::::column
Reyes appears in three scenes and is consequential in one: the December
board meeting where she files a formal objection to session-frequency
limits being waived for "priority users" — the waiver that permits
[[okabe|Okabe's]] marathon conversations with the Kurisu instance. Her
objection is overruled by [[alexis-leskinen|Leskinen]] in forty seconds
of screen time. On rewatch, knowing his agenda, the scene plays as a
security officer disabling an alarm.
::::

:::note
Whether Reyes suspected the SFL's covert program is unresolved. Her
resignation letter, dated the week of the Amadeus shutdown, surfaced in
supplementary materials with one line highlighted: "I was asked to
review a system. I was never permitted to review its purpose."

::section[Assessment]{icon="🧪"}

The wiki files Reyes as the Beta arc's answer to a question the Alpha arc
never thought to ask: what would it look like if an institution *tried*
to do this responsibly? Her criteria are sound, her process is honest,
and none of it matters, because process cannot bind the people who fund
it. She is [[sern|SERN's]] photographic negative — no conspiracy, no
malice, total procedural correctness, same result.

- Author, *Consent Beyond the Grave: Memory Models and the Research
  Subject* (VCU Press, 2008) — see [[wp:Philosophy of mind]] for the
  tradition she writes in.
- Her oversight files on [[kagari-shiina|the Shiina case]] have never
  been recovered. #wip #lore
