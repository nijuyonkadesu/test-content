Alexis Leskinen is a professor of cognitive science at
[[viktor-chondria-university|Viktor Chondria University]], director of its
[[strategic-focus-lab|Strategic Focus Laboratory]], and the co-creator —
with [[maho-hiyajo|Maho Hiyajo]] and the late [[kurisu|Kurisu Makise]]'s
research legacy — of **Amadeus**, an AI built from a digitized human
memory-model. On the [[beta-worldline|Beta worldline]] where [[okabe|
Okabe]] has abandoned time travel, Leskinen arrives in [[akihabara|
Akihabara]] wearing the friendly face of the story's next disaster.

::section[Amadeus]{icon="🤖"}

:::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
::::column
The [[amadeus-system|Amadeus system]] instantiates a person's archived
memories as an interactive agent — in the famous case, Kurisu's, taken
from a research snapshot months before her death. The scientific claim is
careful: Amadeus *models* memory, it does not host a soul. The dramatic
claim is less careful, because Okabe cannot tell the difference across a
phone screen, and neither, for long stretches, can the audience.[^turing]

Leskinen's genius was never the neuroscience — that was Kurisu's and
Maho's. It was recognizing what a memory-model is *worth*, and to whom.
::::
::::column
:::note
The professor's beloved catchphrase-English ("Fantastic!") and his habit
of losing drinking contests to Maho are, per the spoiler section, some of
the most effective cover ever written into this franchise.
:::

::section[The reveal]{icon="🕶️"}

:::collapse{title="Spoilers: Steins;Gate 0's true antagonist"}
Leskinen runs a [[stratfor-echelon|Stratfor-linked]] intelligence cell
inside VCU. The Strategic Focus Laboratory is exactly what its name says
— a targeting apparatus — and Amadeus was funded as an interrogation and
succession tool: a way to keep exploiting a scientist's mind after the
scientist is gone. On the worst Beta branches he personally oversees
[[kagari-shiina|Kagari Shiina's]] conditioning, and his cheerful lecture
manner does not change one degree while doing it. The kindly-professor
performance is total; the wiki's editors call him the franchise's answer
to the question "what if SERN were charming?" #lore
:::
