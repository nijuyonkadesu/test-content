# Organizations

Institutions, agencies, companies, and informal groups that shape the
[[steins-gate-worldline|worldline]] structure of the setting — from
[[sern|SERN's]] overt particle-physics research down to the dojin circle
[[future-gadget-laboratory|meeting above the]] [[radio-kaikan|Radio Kaikan]]
building. Some entries here are public-facing institutions; others,
like [[committee-of-300|the Committee of 300]], are conspiracies whose
existence is only ever inferred from paper trails and secondhand accounts.
Treat anything sourced to a single informant — living or otherwise — with
appropriate skepticism. #lore

For the people who staff, found, infiltrate, or oppose these bodies, see
[[People|Category: People]].
