# People

Every person on this wiki outside the core cast — lab associates, family
members, rivals, informants, and the researchers and field operatives
orbiting [[sern|SERN]] on both sides of the [[steins-gate-worldline|Steins
Gate]] divergence. Dossiers here range from full lab members to
single-appearance contacts; check each page's infobox for affiliation and
last-known worldline status.

For the organizations these people belong to, see [[Organizations|Category:
Organizations]]. For the four founding lab members — [[okabe|Okabe]],
[[kurisu|Kurisu]], [[mayuri|Mayuri]], and [[daru|Daru]] — see
[[Characters|Category: Characters]] instead; this index deliberately
excludes them to avoid duplicate listings.
