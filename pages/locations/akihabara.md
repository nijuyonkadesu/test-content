**Akihabara** — *Akiba* to everyone who has been twice — is Tokyo's
electric town: a district of component shops, anime storefronts, maid
cafes, and arcades stacked vertically until the signage becomes the
architecture. It is the home ground of the
[[future-gadget-lab|Future Gadget Laboratory]] and the stage for nearly
every event this wiki documents. When worldlines shift, ==Akihabara is
where the difference shows first== — and in one infamous case, the
district itself was the difference.

::section[Character]{icon="🏮"}

::::columns{ratio="65-35"}
:::column
Akiba runs on two economies. Above ground: flagship stores, character
goods, billboards that replace themselves seasonally, the crowd flowing
from [[akihabara-station|the station]] down Chuo-dori on foot when the
street closes to cars. Below the signage: the older metabolism of
component stalls and junk shops — bins of salvaged capacitors, one-man
counters that can source a vacuum tube discontinued before the customer
was born. The [[future-gadget-lab|lab]] is a creature of the second
economy; the [[divergence-meter|Divergence Meter]]'s Nixie tubes, the
[[future-gadget-02|Helicam]]'s camera, and most of the FG line's organs
were bought within a ten-minute radius of its door.

The district's texture is why the lab could exist unnoticed for so long:
in Akihabara, a person carrying a raygun-shaped remote control and
twenty meters of coolant hose is ==below the threshold of remark==.
:::

::section[Worldline sensitivity]{icon="🌀"}

Akihabara's present character is *recent* — a layer of otaku commerce
deposited over an older radio-parts district in a single generation. This
made it terrifyingly responsive to D-Mail interference. The lab's
documented worldline shifts altered, at various divergence values: which
stores anchored which corners, whether a landmark
[[radio-kaikan|building]] bore a satellite through its roof, and — at the
deepest recorded excursion — ==whether the otaku layer had happened at
all==. On the 0.33-band worldline, lab members walked out into an
Akihabara of gray electronics wholesalers, the moe signage gone as if
scrubbed, and understood viscerally what the
[[attractor-field|attractor field]] pages mean by "detail." See
[[episode-12|Episode 12]].

| Divergence band | Akihabara's state | Witness |
|---|---|---|
| 0.33 | Radio-town only; otaku culture absent | [[okabe\|Okabe]] |
| 0.40–0.57 | Familiar Electric Town, minor storefront drift | Lab consensus |
| 1.04 ([[steins-gate-worldline\|SG]]) | Familiar, plus one impossible satellite | Everyone, finally |
| 1.13 | Familiar; military presence in later years | See [[beta-worldline]] |

Storefronts that exist only in [[reading-steiner|one man's memory]]: a

cabinet. Okabe's list is in the lab notebook under "places I owe money
in worldlines that no longer exist."
:::

:::collapse{title="A word on crowds"}
Akiba's crowd is the lab's camouflage and its dread. Every SERN tail was
lost in it; one was *spotted* because he stood still in it, which nobody
in Akihabara does. The [[lab-member-handbook|handbook]]'s tradecraft
section is one line: "Move like you're late for a preorder."
:::
