The **Future Gadget Laboratory** is a rented second-floor walkup in
[[akihabara|Akihabara]] where [[okabe|Rintaro Okabe]] and a small,
fiercely loyal membership build joke inventions — and where, in the summer
everything changed, they accidentally built the world's second time
machine. It is a clubroom, a workshop, a safehouse, and at various points
a battlefield; the [[episode-guide|episode guide]] barely leaves it for
whole stretches, because for this story the lab *is* the world.

::section[Premises]{icon="🏠"}

::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
:::column
The lab occupies one open room plus a kitchenette, a bathroom, and a
steep stair to the roof. Furnishing is archaeological: a sagging sofa
(sleep rated, two persons, diagonal), the main workbench, a CRT
television that predates most members, and shelving whose lower strata
have not been observed since founding. The [[phone-microwave|PhoneWave]]
holds the kitchenette counter; the [[divergence-meter|Divergence Meter]]
sits on the anomaly shelf beside the [[gel-banana|sealed specimens]]; the
wall hooks hold [[future-gadget-01|FG #1]] and whichever umbrella nobody
has reclaimed.

Below, at street level, hums the [[braun-tube-workshop|Braun Tube
Workshop]], whose proprietor doubles as landlord and involuntary
first-responder to everything the lab drops, floods, or detonates. The
relationship is best described as ==weaponized patience==, in both
directions.

::section[A tour]{icon="🚪"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="Main room"}
Workbench, sofa, CRT, whiteboard bearing the [[attractor-field|valley
diagram]] in its fourth redrawing and one corner of ink that doesn't
erase. The floor's center is kept clear by standing order — a habit from
the [[future-gadget-02|Helicam]] era that outlived the flights.
:::
:::tab{label="Kitchenette"}
The PhoneWave's domain. House rules laminated above it: no metal in the
drum, no bananas *near* it, photograph the [[divergence-meter|meter]]
before anything ambitious. The rice situation is governed by the
[[future-gadget-05|Kerberos Tap]]'s third head, currently vacant.
:::
:::tab{label="Roof"}
Laundry line, two folding chairs, and the best view of
[[akihabara|Akiba]]'s signage anyone gets for this rent. Where lab
members go to say the things that don't get said in the main room.
Several of the [[episode-guide|guide]]'s quietest scenes happen here.
:::
:::tab{label="The stair"}
Steep, loud, and the lab's true security system — nobody has ever reached
the door unannounced. The seventh step's creak is load-bearing to at
least two plot developments. #lore
:::

:::note
The lab has been searched twice, bugged once (found: behind the CRT,
where the dust betrayed it), and defended at cost that this wiki
documents on the worldline pages rather than here. It has never once
been voluntarily tidied.
:::

::section[Culture]{icon="🎌"}

Membership is by number, conferred by Okabe with full ceremony including
the [[future-gadget-03|Cyalume Saber]] ignition. Duties are enumerated in
the [[lab-member-handbook|Lab Member Handbook]]; in practice the binding
obligations are three: answer your phone, ==never let Okabe debrief a
worldline shift alone==, and buy your share of Dk Pepper. Meetings open
whenever Okabe declares them open and adjourn when [[mayuri|Mayuri]] says
"tuturu" in the specific descending tone that means dinner.

:::collapse{title="On the rent"}
Rent is paid monthly, in cash, downstairs, in an envelope Mayuri
decorates because "Mr. Braun pretends to hate it but keeps them." Arrears
have historically been settled in kind: CRT deliveries carried, shop
watched during hospital visits, one memorable fortnight of
[[future-gadget-04|Moad Snake]] rental. The economics do not add up and
are not meant to. #lore
:::
