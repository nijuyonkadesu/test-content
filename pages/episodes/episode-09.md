# Episode 09: Chaos Theory Homeostasis II

Faris's wish rewrites a decade of city planning. Okabe steps out of the lab
into an [[akihabara|Akihabara]] with no maid cafes, no anime billboards, and
no reason to have ever welcomed people like him.

:::::columns{ratio="65-35"}
::::column

## Synopsis

[[faris-nyannyan|Faris NyanNyan]] — owner-mascot of
[[may-queen-nyan-nyan|May Queen Nyan-Nyan]] and undefeated champion of the
Rai-Net card scene — asks for her D-Mail: a message to her past self, ten
years back, contents private. Okabe grants it. The lurch comes.

The [[akihabara|Akihabara]] Okabe walks out into is *electronics-district*
Akihabara, the pre-2000s version: gray storefronts, component bins,
no [[may-queen-nyan-nyan|May Queen]], no Rai-Net, no moe culture at all.
Faris's father — dead in every previous line — is alive, and the family's
influence steered the district's development down another path entirely.[^1]

[[mayuri|Mayuri]]'s cosplay circle never formed. [[daru|Daru]]'s favorite
shops never opened. The lab itself survives, but the city around it has
been swapped out from under everyone — and only Okabe grieves a version of
the town that, here, never existed.

:::note
Divergence: $0.456903 \to 0.409431$. The largest *cultural* shift of the
series from the smallest stated wish — the Homeostasis trilogy's thesis in
one move.
:::

The episode's ethical hinge: Faris is *happier* here. Her father is alive.
She remembers nothing of the other city — except that, when pressed, she
tells Okabe about a dream of a cat-eared cafe, and thanks him for a promise
he made in a worldline she cannot remember.[^2]

Okabe does not undo this line in this episode. He files it, in his ledger,
under debts. The undoing — and Faris's own choice about it — waits until
the back half of the season, where each Homeostasis wish is paid down in
reverse order.

::section[Continuity]{icon="🔗"}

- Faris displays partial dream-memory of pre-shift events — the second
  hint (after Okabe) that Reading Steiner admits degrees. See
  [[alpha-worldline#Reading Steiner|Reading Steiner]].
- The IBN 5100 remains missing in this line; its shrine trail from
  [[episode-04]] is doubly dead here.
- Mayuri still owns the metal [[upa|Upa]]. Some things converge.
- Rai-Net's absence removes a minor character's entire subplot — the
  drama CD covers what happened to the tournament arc.

:::collapse{title="Spoilers — what the D-Mail said"}
Ten words to her past self, timed to the night her father left for the
airport. He took a later flight. The series reveals the text only in the
undo episode, and it is worse to hear than any speculation: it was a
completely ordinary message. That is the point.
:::
