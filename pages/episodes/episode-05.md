# Episode 05: Starmine Rendezvous

The IBN 5100 comes home to the lab, carried up eight flights of stairs by
four people who have no idea they are carrying the season's fuse.

:::::columns{ratio="65-35"}
::::column

## Synopsis

Okabe's shrine lead pays off. [[ruka-urushibara|Ruka Urushibara]] — gentle,
soft-spoken, and endlessly patient with Okabe's Hououin Kyouma routine —
retrieves the dusty IBN 5100 from the [[yanabayashi-shrine|Yanabayashi
Shrine]] storeroom with the blessing of Ruka's father, on the condition that
Okabe stop teaching Ruka sword-fighting poses in the shrine yard.[^1]

Getting a 25-kilogram vintage computer across [[akihabara|Akihabara]] and up
to the [[future-gadget-lab|lab]] takes the whole episode's middle act and
most of its comedy budget. [[mr-braun|Mr. Braun]], the landlord, watches them
haul it past his CRT shop and mutters that ==real machines have tubes==.

With the machine installed, [[daru|Daru]] and [[kurisu|Kurisu]] begin
decoding the stolen SERN archive from [[episode-03]]. The oldest records
confirm the worst reading of the [[jellyman-report|Jellyman's Report]]:
SERN has been receiving — and disposing of — human test subjects since
before the lab existed.

:::warning
From this episode on, the lab possesses both halves of a catastrophe:
a working D-Mail device *and* proof of what SERN does to people who have one.
:::

Ruka is introduced through patience: holding a pose Okabe assigned, hours
after Okabe forgot assigning it. The episode trusts the audience to find
this funny *and* quietly sad, which is the register Ruka's whole arc — the
D-Mail in [[episode-10]] and its undoing in [[episode-11]] — will live in.

Fans mark this episode as the start of the series' habit of hiding its
heaviest future beats inside its lightest episodes.[^2]

::section[Continuity]{icon="🔗"}

- The IBN 5100's storeroom location matches Titor's post in [[episode-02]]
  exactly — the first hard confirmation that "Titor" has local knowledge.
- Mr. Braun's CRT shop, below the lab, becomes significant in
  [[episode-12]] — remember what he says about tubes.
- Mayuri's part-time job at [[may-queen-nyan-nyan|May Queen Nyan-Nyan]] is
  shown for the first time; [[faris-nyannyan|Faris]] cameos.
- The decode confirms the phrase *human is dead, mismatch* from
  [[episode-03#The SERN hack|the SERN hack]].

:::collapse{title="Spoilers — the shrine's second secret"}
The IBN 5100 was donated to the shrine by a time traveler. The donor's
identity — and why the machine was placed *there*, findable by exactly this
lab — is part of the loop closed in [[episode-22|the finale]]. The shrine
scenes reward a second viewing frame by frame.
:::
