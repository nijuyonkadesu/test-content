**Future Gadget #3**, the **Cyalume Saber**, is a sword-shaped emergency
light: a polycarbonate blade filled with the two-part chemiluminescent
fluid used in glow sticks, ignited — Okabe's verb — by a twist of the hilt
that ruptures the inner cartridge and mixes the reagents. For forty
minutes thereafter, the bearer holds a softly teal sword that cannot cut,
burn, or meaningfully illuminate, and is nonetheless the most-borrowed
gadget the [[future-gadget-lab|lab]] has ever produced.

::section[Chemistry]{icon="🧪"}

::::columns{ratio="60-40"}
:::column
The blade's glow is standard peroxyoxalate chemiluminescence: hydrogen
peroxide oxidizes a diphenyl oxalate ester, and the reaction energy
excites a fluorescent dye that emits as it relaxes. No filament, no
battery in the blade itself, no heat worth mentioning — the reaction runs
at room temperature, which is the entire reason it can live inside a
sealed plastic sword without the sword becoming a hazard.

[[daru|Daru]]'s contribution was the **cartridge**: a glass ampoule of
peroxide suspended in the ester-dye mix, sized so a hilt-twist snaps it
cleanly. Refills are hand-filled at the lab bench with fluid bought in
bulk from a party supplier in [[akihabara|Akihabara]], at a unit cost
[[mayuri|Mayuri]]'s ledger records as "cheaper than snacks, brighter than
nothing."
:::
:::column
**Dye options**

- Teal (canonical)
- Red ("Sith mode," Daru)
- Yellow #stub — reacts too fast,
  burns out in 9 minutes
- Ultraviolet — invisible, philosophically
  troubling, discontinued
:::
::::

The v2 cartridge's ==15 lumens== deserves context: it is brighter than any
commercial glow stick and dimmer than every flashlight ever sold. In a
blackout it renders a room "navigable but melancholy." At a convention it
photographs beautifully. These are, the lab has concluded, the correct
priorities.

:::tip
Twisting the hilt *slowly* mixes the reagents unevenly, producing a blade
that glows in drifting bands — unintended, unfixable, and by broad
agreement the best thing the gadget does. Lab members call the effect
==worldline marbling==, because of course they do.
:::

::section[Service history]{icon="⚔️"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="The blackout"}
During the building-wide power failure that also felled the
[[braun-tube-workshop|CRT shop]] downstairs, three ignited Sabers lit the
lab's stairwell for the duration. This is the gadget's only unambiguous
operational success and it is cited constantly.
:::
:::tab{label="Comima duels"}
Convention choreography revealed the structural rating's meaning: two
Sabers meeting at speed produce one Saber and one teal spill. The fluid is
nontoxic and stains everything. See the [[may-queen-nyan-nyan|May
Queen]]'s tablecloth, which still fluoresces under UV.
:::
:::tab{label="Ceremonial"}
A Saber is ignited at every lab member induction — a tradition begun at
member #004's swearing-in and maintained since, cartridge supply
permitting. The [[lab-member-handbook|handbook]] lists it under
"obligations, non-negotiable."
:::
::::

:::collapse{title="Why not LEDs?"}
An LED blade would be brighter, rechargeable, and permanent — and was
vetoed by Okabe on the record: *"A sword that can be switched off is a
prop. A sword that dies in forty minutes is a story."* The lab notebook
preserves a rebuttal from [[kurisu|Kurisu]] consisting of the word
"battery" underlined four times, then, in different ink, "…fine, it's a
story."
:::

::section[See also]{icon="🔗"}

The underlying reaction is documented at [[wp:Glow stick]]; the lab's
cartridge design adds nothing to the chemistry and everything to the
theater. FG #3 sits between the [[future-gadget-01|Bit Particle Cannon]]'s
pure pageantry and the [[future-gadget-04|Moad Snake]]'s pure utility —
the first gadget where the joke and the function are the same thing. A
proposed two-handed variant, the [[cyalume-claymore|Cyalume Claymore]],
exists only as a red link and a whiteboard sketch of Daru holding it.
