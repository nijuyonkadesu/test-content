**Future Gadget #4**, the **Moad Snake**, is a desktop ultrasonic
humidifier built into an articulated gooseneck shaped — deliberately,
lovingly — like a snake. The head aims; the coiled body is the water
reservoir; the mist emerges from the mouth in slow, dramatic curls. It is
the only Future Gadget in *continuous daily service* at the
[[future-gadget-lab|lab]], where Tokyo winters and a room full of CRTs
otherwise produce air with the humidity of a kiln.

::section[The name]{icon="🔤"}

The gadget was to be called the **Mood Snake**, for the ambience. The
label maker had other plans. By the time anyone noticed, "Moad" was
painted on the chassis, written in the lab ledger, and — decisively —
funnier. A retroactive backronym was commissioned and the winning entry,
==**M**ist **O**riented **A**mbience **D**evice==, is now the official
line whenever anyone asks, which they always do.[^1]

::section[Mechanism]{icon="⚙️"}

::::columns{ratio="65-35"}
:::column
An ultrasonic transducer at the base of the throat oscillates at 1.7 MHz,
shattering the water surface into a cold fog of droplets a few microns
across; a whisper-quiet fan walks the fog up the neck and out the mouth.
The neck's segments are standard loc-line coolant hose, which holds any
pose — the lab's default is "regarding the divergence meter with
suspicion."

The engineering problem worth documenting is **head droop**: a water-
carrying neck gets heavier as the reservoir feeds forward. Daru's fix
routes the feed line through the neck's neutral axis and moves 80% of
reservoir volume into the base coils, dropping the moment on the neck by
two-thirds. FG #4 is thereby the only Future Gadget with a free-body
diagram in its documentation.
:::
:::column
**Poses, canonical**

- Sentinel (default)
- Regarding the [[divergence-meter|meter]]
- Dramatic S-curve (visitors)
- Down-periscope (storage)
- "Do not disturb" (aimed at door)
:::

:::note
The mist is *cold* — ultrasonic humidification adds no heat. First-time
visitors who put a hand in the "snake's breath" expecting warmth
invariably flinch, a reaction the lab logbook tracks under the heading
"Moad bites" (current count: 34).
:::

::section[Service record]{icon="🗓️"}

::::tabs
:::tab{label="Winter duty"}
Runs 8+ hours daily from November through March. Credited by
[[daru|Daru]] with ending the static-shock feud of the lab's first
winter, in which every doorknob was a betrayal.
:::
:::tab{label="CRT stewardship"}
[[braun-tube-workshop|Mr. Braun]] borrowed FG #4 for a week to test
whether humidified air reduced dust attraction on his shop's tube faces.
Results: inconclusive on dust, conclusive on ambience. He built his own
copy rather than admit he wanted one.
:::
:::tab{label="Incidents"}
One (1): a lab member repositioned the head mid-fill and irrigated the
[[ibn-5100|IBN 5100]]'s bench from 60 cm. The IBN, sealed like a
battleship, was unharmed. The incident report is one sentence: "The snake
was aimed; the snake discharged."
:::
::::

:::collapse{title="Kurisu's assessment"}
[[kurisu|Kurisu]]'s lab-notebook review, quoted in full: "It's a
humidifier. It works. The snake adds nothing." Appended below in Okabe's
hand: "The snake adds *everything*." Appended below that, in Kurisu's,
after a visible gap: "…the snake adds something."
:::

::section[See also]{icon="🔗"}

Ultrasonic humidification is covered at [[wp:Humidifier]]. FG #4 marks the
lab's quiet maturation: after the [[future-gadget-01|Cannon]]'s theater
and the [[future-gadget-02|Helicam]]'s ambition, gadget #4 simply *works*,
every day, at the one job it has — a standard later gadgets are measured
against and mostly fail. Its only rival for practicality is the
[[phone-microwave|gadget that broke causality]], and that one caused
measurably more trouble.
