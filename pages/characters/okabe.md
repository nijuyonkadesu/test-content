The self-proclaimed mad scientist "Hououin Kyouma". Founder of the
[[index|Future Gadget Laboratory]] and Lab Member No. 001. Discovers that the
lab's Phone Microwave can send a D-Mail to the past, draws the attention of
SERN, and spends the rest of the story paying for it.

He works closely with [[kurisu|Kurisu Makise]] and protects [[mayuri|Mayuri Shiina]].
His attempts to reach the [[Steins Gate worldline]] drive the whole plot.
