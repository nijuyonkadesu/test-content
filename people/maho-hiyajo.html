Maho Hiyajo is a senior researcher at Viktor Chondria University, co-creator of the Amadeus memory-model AI, and the person who knew Kurisu Makise best in the years America had her. On the Beta worldline she arrives in Japan carrying two things: the Amadeus build containing her dead friend's memories, and a grief she has decided to call a research program.

🎹Salieri

Maho's self-mythology runs through Antonio Salieri — the capable musician history remembers mostly for standing next to Mozart. She was Kurisu's senpai: older, earlier into the program, and then, permanently, second. The show treats her framing with more respect than she treats herself; the text is clear that ==Amadeus exists because Salieri built it==, and that Kurisu knew exactly which of them could.1

Watch her hands, not her dialogue. Maho states her positions calmly and then plays piano like she is trying to break it. The animators used the piano as her polygraph.

🗼In Akihabara

Sent to Japan for the Amadeus lecture circuit alongside Professor Leskinen, Maho collides with the Future Gadget Laboratory and with Okabe — the one person whose grief for Kurisu matches hers and whose reasons cannot be spoken aloud. Their wary alliance becomes the emotional spine of the Beta arc: two executors of the same estate, comparing inventories.

She is also, notably, the first outsider to reconstruct the truth of the Phone Microwave from evidence alone — Kurisu's surviving files, plus the lab's conspicuous silences.2

🌐Across the worldlines

Central. She guards the Amadeus data through the SFL betrayal, burns what has to burn, and earns a place on the short list of people Okabe trusts with worldline truth.
