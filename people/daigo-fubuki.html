Daigo Fubuki, codename F1, is the reconstructed identity of the Rounders field commander for the Far East sector — the tier of the hierarchy above the handlers, the man to whom "FB" reported. He never appears on screen. His existence is inferred entirely from the recovered message logs of the people beneath him, which makes this page the wiki's most heavily disclaimed biography and, editors would argue, its most honest picture of how SERN actually works.

Authenticity note. Every fact on this page is a reconstruction from the FB message archive and two SOC routing headers recovered in operation-verdandi. The name "Daigo Fubuki" itself may be another alias in a chain of them. Treat the infobox as a hypothesis with a picture. #lore #stub

🗄️The collector

F1's traffic shows one obsession: vintage computers. The IBN 5100 retrieval order that drives half the Alpha arc originates from his desk, but it sits in a longer pattern — recovered manifests show SOC teams quietly acquiring pre-1980 hardware across three continents since at least 2005.1 The lab's eventual theory: SERN was not looking for the IBN 5100. It was looking for all of them, because any surviving unit could decode what the lab decoded.

📡Command style

Read end to end, F1's side of the FB correspondence is a portrait of institutional evil at its most banal: no threats, no ideology, just deadlines. Assets are "inventory." The order to cut M4 loose is eleven words long and misspells her codename. The wiki reproduces none of the messages directly — the archive's legal status is disputed — but the pattern they document is why this page exists.

On Alpha branches that reach 2036, SOC routing headers place F1's successor organization inside the occupation government's registry directorate. The Rounders never disband; they get promoted. See Suzuha's page for the world that follows.
